Alsaer

Alsaer is a free, open-source Android app for Muslims: prayer times and qibla, the Quran, daily adhkar and Hisn al-Muslim, hadith, worship and habit tracking, and reminders. Your data stays on your phone. Linking Google Drive is optional and saves a backup copy of your data in your own account.
